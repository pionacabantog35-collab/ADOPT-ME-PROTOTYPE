# ADOPT-ME-PROTOTYPE


Adopt Me: Pet Adoption System (Prototype)

Adopt Me is a mobile-first web app that connects people who want to adopt a pet with people who have a pet that needs a new home. It is designed to be as simple as scrolling a social media feed.

How it works

When the app opens, the user sees the logo, a friendly cartoon cat. Tapping it leads to two choices: Adopt or Post for adoption.

Adopt. Available pets appear in a vertical feed, one pet per screen, like a dating app or Facebook. Each post shows the pet's photo, name, age and a few traits. When the user taps the heart on a pet they like, the app shows the poster's contact details: a phone number and a Messenger link. The adopter can then contact the owner directly to arrange the adoption.

Post for adoption. A user who wants to rehome a pet uploads a photo and the pet's basic details, then answers a short health check about injuries, symptoms and activity level.

If the pet seems sick or injured, it is not listed. The app shows the nearest animal rescues and veterinary clinics, sorted by distance, with options to call or get directions.
If the pet seems healthy, the user adds their contact details and the pet is posted to the Adopt feed.

Purpose and benefits

Makes adoption easier and faster for both owners and adopters.
Helps keep sick or injured animals from being listed before they get care.
Points owners to nearby rescue and veterinary help.
Uses a familiar scrolling and "like" interface that needs no training.

Prototype scope. This version demonstrates the user flow only. The health check is question-based, the sample vets and contacts are placeholders, and posts are not saved. Future versions could add AI photo screening, real location and map data, user accounts, and a database.
